Criteria: (a) time to a working browser MVP in three months, (b) later native linking into WinUI 3 / SwiftUI / GTK-or-Qt, (c) toolchain setup on Windows, (d) mature image and PDF codecs for the browser.

Source-backed notes on trade-offs:
- The rustwasm GitHub organisation was sunset: wasm-bindgen moved to the `wasm-bindgen` organisation with new maintainers; wasm-pack was listed for archiving and now lives at `wasm-bindgen/wasm-pack` (v0.15.0, 2026-05-15, pushed 2026-10-21). Both are active. Sources: https://blog.rust-lang.org/inside-rust/2025/07/21/sunsetting-the-rustwasm-github-org (2025-07-21), https://github.com/wasm-bindgen/wasm-pack (retrieved 2026-10-22).
- A Rust forum thread frames `wasm32-unknown-emscripten` as the legacy route and notes most Rust web-platform crates support only wasm-bindgen, which is incompatible with Emscripten (community opinion, 2024): https://users.rust-lang.org/t/wasm32-unknown-unknown-vs-wasm32-unknown-emscripten-in-2024/112183. No 2026 independent head-to-head comparison was found; benchmark articles found were not reliable and are not cited.
- Sources disagree on nothing material, but the evidence for (b) on GTK/Qt and for JPEG quality in pure Rust is missing.
